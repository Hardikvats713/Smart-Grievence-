# 11 — Decisions and open questions

## Confirmed project direction
- Product name: Smart Grievance.
- Current phase: Smart Grievance only.
- Client: mobile application only.
- Proposed mobile framework: Flutter.
- Visual direction: warm ivory, deep teal, restrained ember and mint sage.
- Typeface: Poppins.
- Glassmorphism: selective, primarily interactive buttons.
- Citizen bottom navigation: Home, My Grievances, Map, Profile.

## Requires owner confirmation
1. Final logo source file and permitted variants.
2. Final grievance categories and subcategories.
3. Required and optional submission fields.
4. Supported languages and translations.
5. OTP provider and authentication policy.
6. Geographic jurisdiction and administrative hierarchy.
7. Department and officer directory source.
8. Role permissions and approval authority.
9. SLA and escalation policy, including whether 7/15/30 days apply.
10. Citizen reopening and appeal rules.
11. Evidence file types, size/count limits and retention.
12. Public map visibility and location precision.
13. Privacy notice, consent and data retention requirements.
14. Hosting, storage and notification providers.
15. Whether AI classification is enabled in the first release.

Do not fill these gaps with invented assumptions. Record decisions here with date, owner and rationale.
